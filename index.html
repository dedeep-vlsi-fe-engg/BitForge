Milestones

The inventions our tools stand on, in order.

1947

The transistor is demonstrated at Bell Labs by Bardeen, Brattain and Shockley.

1958

The integrated circuit: Jack Kilby at Texas Instruments; Robert Noyce follows with a silicon IC in 1959.

1963

CMOS logic is invented by Frank Wanlass at Fairchild — today’s chips are almost all CMOS.

1965

Moore’s law: Gordon Moore predicts transistor counts will keep doubling.

1971

Intel 4004, the first commercial microprocessor, with about 2,300 transistors.

1973

SPICE circuit simulation is released at UC Berkeley.

1984

Verilog is created at Gateway Design Automation; it becomes IEEE 1364 in 1995.

1985

The first FPGA, the Xilinx XC2064, ships with 64 configurable logic blocks.

About BitForge
